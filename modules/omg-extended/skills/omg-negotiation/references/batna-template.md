# BATNA Template — Pre-Negotiation Worksheet

Fill this template BEFORE every significant negotiation, and recalculate at every 2-round interval during the negotiation. Built on Fisher/Ury (BATNA), Shell (three-axis leverage), Bazerman (cognitive bias mitigation), and Malhotra (BATNA expansion).

**Output location:** `{deal-folder}/internal/negotiation-prep-{counterparty}-{YYMMDD}.md`

---

## Part 1 — BATNA & reservation price

**Strength assessment:**
- [ ] Multiple credible alternatives → strong BATNA

### 1.5 ZOPA (Zone of Possible Agreement)

**Overlap between your range and their range:**

```
Yours:   [______reservation______target______maximum______]
Theirs:  [_____maximum______target______reservation______]
ZOPA:    [_______OVERLAP_______]
```

- [ ] **Clear ZOPA** → deal likely; focus on getting the best within ZOPA
- [ ] **Borderline ZOPA** → need value creation (multi-dimensional trades, pie expansion)
- [ ] **No ZOPA detected** → either find new dimensions, or walk

## Part 10 — Mid-negotiation BATNA recalculation

**Recalculate at:**
- [ ] After Round 4 (mid-game)
- [ ] After Round 6 (late-middle)
- [ ] Before final-round close

### Recalculation prompts:

1. Has THEIR behavior changed your assessment of THEIR BATNA?
2. Has YOUR situation changed (new alternative, lost alternative)?
3. If you were starting fresh today, would you accept current terms?
4. Has ZOPA changed (collapsed, expanded, shifted)?

If answer to #3 is "no" but you're tempted to sign — you're in sunk-cost trap. See `failure-modes.md` Section 5.

## Filled example (for Alfa context)

This is what a filled-in template might look like for the Alfa v3 → v4 negotiation:

### 1.1 BATNA
- Option 1: Hire in-house sales lead (~$200K base + $50K equity grant)
- Option 2: Use a different sales partner (Yota, Helsinki-based gaming sales firm — proxy contact via LinkedIn)
- Option 3: Self-serve sales for first 12 months, hire later when revenue justifies

**Strength: Moderate.** Not desperate; can survive without Alfa.

### 1.2 Reservation price
- Equity: 8% maximum (was 10% in v3)
- Vesting: 12-month total minimum (no shortening)
- Concentration cap: 35% maximum (firm; can't drop)
- Key Person: must remain (critical accountability)
- Non-compete: 18 months minimum
- Stickiness: 6 months minimum

### 2.1 Positive leverage
1. Founder personal holdings funding the equity (no other-shareholder dilution for Alfa)
2. Track record in mobile gaming (Apple/Google certifications, 5+ shipped titles)
3. Founder's HK/Vietnam network (potentially valuable to Alfa's Turkish portfolio)

### 2.2 Negative leverage
1. Walk and self-fund (genuine BATNA option)
2. Competitor mention: Helsinki-based Yota does similar work

### 2.3 Normative leverage
1. FAST advisor agreement standard (4-month cliff, 12-month vest) — Alfa proposing extends well beyond
2. Korean publishers' standard advisor equity range: 0.5-1.5% of company. Alfa's 10% is at extreme top.

### 4.1 Red lines
1. Concentration cap remains in some form (single client < 40%) — without this, fundamental portfolio fragility
2. Key Person clause remains — without this, no accountability for who actually does the work
3. Definition of "Qualifying Client" stays at 12-month commitment minimum — this defines the entire economic structure

### 5.2 Opening anchor (v4)
| Dimension | Opening | Justification |
|-----------|---------|---------------|
| Total equity | 9% (down from 10% in v3) | Counterparty wants more, we tighten as response to their definition attacks |
| WCP target Gate 3 | 18 (up from 16) | Compensation for accepting some short-commitment client credit |
| Stickiness window | 7 months (down from 8) | Conceding |
| Concentration cap | 35% (tightened from 40%) | Defended |
| Key Person clause | Maintained but with named-substitute provision | Compromise |

### 6.1 Round 1 concession (largest)
- Concede: Drop 40% gross margin monitoring (already non-gating; easy give)
- Demand: WCP target increases from 16 → 18 at Gate 3

### 7. Top 3 patterns to expect
1. **Accept volume, attack definitions** (already deployed in v3) — counter: definition discipline + multi-dimensional bundles
2. **Growth-stage rhetoric bundle** (already deployed) — counter: reverse the frame, bundle their bundle
3. **Disclosure deflection** (already deployed) — counter: demand 4 portfolio company names by next round

### 9.1 Walk-away
**Trigger:** If Alfa demands removing concentration cap entirely AND removing Key Person clause AND keeping current short-commitment client credit (any 2 of 3).

**Script:** "I think we need different terms. Let's pause for 2 weeks and see if either side has new thinking. If not, I'll proceed with [in-house hire / Yota] and we can revisit if our paths cross again."

**Pause:** 5 days minimum.

**Re-engagement:** Wait for Ozgur to reach out. If he does, recommit at ORIGINAL v3 terms (not v4) — let them feel the cost of pushing too far.
