# Mode 4 — `rationale` (term rationale documentation)

For each major clause, produce a 4-part analysis:

1. **Why it exists** — what problem does this clause solve?
2. **What Company gets** — the protection or economic right.
3. **What Partner gets** — the reciprocal protection, economic right, or risk reduction.
4. **Tension axis** — which way pressure pushes this clause in negotiation.

Output lives at `{scope}/internal/term-rationale-EN.md`.

## Output template per clause

```markdown
## §X — {clause title}

**Why it exists:** {1–2 sentences on the underlying problem}

**What Company gets:** {specific economic right or protection}

**What Partner gets:** {reciprocal right — even if smaller}

**Tension axis:** {which direction each side will push, and the defensible middle ground}

**Scenario-specific note** (if applicable): {e.g., "In ARR-based, X works differently because..."}
```

## Also include in the rationale doc

- **Divergence table** — for multi-scenario contracts, a table showing each clause × scenario × what differs.
- **Who-wins-where matrix** — a 2-column matrix of clauses vs. winner (Company / Partner / neutral) to surface net-posture at a glance.

## Discipline note

The rationale doc is **internal**, not for sending. If the counterparty asks for rationale, produce a counterparty-facing summary (1 page, no tension-axis section, no internal posture commentary) — not the full doc.
