# Board Quarterly Deck — Template

10–15 slide outline. Target: 30-minute presentation + 30-minute working discussion.

---

## Slide 1 — Cover

- Company logo
- Quarter + year (e.g., "Q1 2026 Board Review")
- Meeting date
- Attendees (board + execs)

## Slide 2 — Agenda

- Executive summary (5 min)
- Financial performance (10 min)
- KPI dashboard + risks (10 min)
- Strategic initiatives (15 min)
- Decisions needed (10 min)
- Discussion + executive session (variable)

## Slide 3 — Executive Summary

Three bullets.

- **Performance:** [one-line on whether we're on track]
- **Risk:** [biggest concern right now]
- **Ask:** [most important decision we need from the board today]

## Slide 4 — Q[X] Financial Performance

```
                  Q[X]      Plan      Δ      YTD      Plan     Δ
Revenue         $  XXk    $XXk    +X%    $XXXk    $XXXk    +X%
Gross margin    XX.X%     XX.X%   +X pp   XX.X%    XX.X%    +X pp
Operating inc   $  XXk    $XXk    -X%    $XXk     $XXk     -X%
Runway           XX mo.                  (current)
```

One sentence commentary on the quarter.

## Slide 5 — P&L Trend (6+ months)

Line chart: Revenue, Cost, Net — with plan reference line.

- Highlight inflection points (e.g., "Feb: first profitable month")
- Annotate one-time items
- Include trailing 12-month sparkline if company is >18mo old

## Slide 6 — KPI Dashboard

Grid of 6–8 key metrics with RAG (Red/Amber/Green) status:

```
| Metric                    | Value | Target | Status | Trend |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue growth YoY        | +42%  | +50%   | 🟡     | ▁▃▅▇  |
| Gross margin %            | 62%   | 60%    | 🟢     | ▅▆▇▇  |
| Runway months             | 16    | ≥12    | 🟢     | ▇▇▆▇  |
| Burn multiple             | 1.8x  | <1.5x  | 🟡     | ▆▅▃▂  |
| Top-3 client concentration| 68%   | <50%   | 🔴     | ▅▆▇▆  |
| Rule of 40                | 38    | ≥40    | 🟡     | ▂▃▅▆  |
| Headcount                 | 13    | 13     | 🟢     | ▃▅▆▇  |
| Employee retention %      | 100%  | ≥90%   | 🟢     | ▇▇▇▇  |
```

Pick 6–8 that matter most for your stage/model.

## Slide 7 — Revenue Breakdown

Stacked bar chart: monthly revenue by business unit, 6 months.

- Commentary on mix shift
- Call out any unit >60% of revenue as concentration risk

## Slide 8 — Risk Dashboard

Risk heatmap or bulleted list with severity:

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Top-1 client concentration | High | High | Diversify: 3 new clients signed Q1 | CEO |
| Key engineer departure | Low | High | Retention bonuses + backup hires | CTO |
| FX volatility (VND/USD) | Med | Med | Fixed-rate policy + quarterly review | CFO |
| Competitive threat ([name]) | Med | Med | Feature gap analysis this quarter | Head of Product |

Always list top 3–5 risks. Mitigation + owner is mandatory.

## Slide 9 — Strategic Initiatives

Status of top 3 bets. RAG status per initiative.

```
Initiative                       Status    Budget   Timeline  Owner
Scale Playable Labs to 20 heads  🟢 On track  $XXXk    Q2       CEO
Launch Game X (in-house title)   🟡 At risk   $XXXk    Q3       Head of Game
Hire head of UA                  🔴 Behind    $XXXk    Q1 → Q2  CEO
```

## Slide 10 — Cash & Runway Scenarios

Chart: cash on hand projected forward 18 months under 3 scenarios.

- **Base:** current growth + current cost
- **Bull:** plan growth + plan cost
- **Bear:** flat growth + cost +3%/mo

Call out:
- Default alive / default dead under each
- When we'd need to raise
- Trigger thresholds for bear-case actions

## Slide 11 — Headcount & Org

- Current headcount, open roles, attrition
- Plan for next quarter
- Any org restructuring notes

## Slide 12 — Decisions Needed

Binary or multi-choice decisions. Board members vote here.

1. **[Decision 1]:** [specific question] — management recommends [option X]
2. **[Decision 2]:** [specific question]
3. **[Authorization]:** [e.g., board approval for hiring CFO, authorizing $X of non-routine spend]

## Slide 13 — Appendix Placeholder (detailed financials, org chart, pipeline, competitor map)

## Best practices

1. **Preserve the fiduciary frame.** Board is legally responsible for corporate oversight. Never hide material information.
2. **Lead with risk.** Founders tend to bury risk at the end. Surface it up front.
3. **Balance numbers and narrative.** Pure numbers = no context. Pure narrative = no rigor.
4. **Send pre-read 48h before meeting.** Never read slides in the meeting; use the time for discussion.
5. **Record decisions in minutes.** Every decision on slide 12 → board minutes with vote tally.
6. **Executive session.** Last 15–30 min is management-excluded. Chair runs it. Used for candid discussion without bias.

## Anti-patterns

1. **"Everything is great."** Board sees through it. Trust erodes.
2. **Dense data slides.** If a slide has >7 rows of numbers, it's a handout, not a slide. Split.
3. **No owner on action items.** Decisions with no owner = decisions not made.
4. **Forgetting last quarter's action items.** Always show status update on prior decisions.
5. **Showing forecast without showing prior forecast accuracy.** How close was last quarter's forecast? Show hit-rate.

## Checklist

- [ ] Pre-read sent 48h before meeting
- [ ] All metrics reconciled to accounting system
- [ ] Top 3 risks surfaced, each with mitigation + owner
- [ ] Strategic initiatives with RAG status
- [ ] Cash scenarios (base/bull/bear) with default-alive indicator
- [ ] Decisions slide with clear binary/multi-choice options
- [ ] Prior-quarter action items status
- [ ] Forecast accuracy retrospective (last quarter)
- [ ] Executive session scheduled
- [ ] Materials archived in board dataroom post-meeting
